FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Is PaidUp really free? ▼
Yes, genuinely. The free plan gives you 5 active invoices, all three email tiers, late fee calculation, and the full dashboard. No credit card. No trial that expires. Free means free. We make money from Pro users who need unlimited invoices and auto-send.
Where is my data stored? ▼
Right in your browser's local storage. Your invoice data never leaves your device on the free plan — we don't have servers storing your financial information. You can export everything as a JSON file anytime. Pro auto-send features use encrypted transmission but never store your email credentials.
Can I cancel Pro anytime? ▼
Absolutely. Cancel with one click from your account, no questions asked. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your billing period. Lifetime plan holders never need to cancel — it's yours forever.
Does PaidUp actually send the emails for me? ▼
On the free plan, PaidUp generates the emails and you copy-paste them into your own email client. On Pro, you can connect your email and PaidUp will auto-send escalating follow-ups on a schedule you set. The emails come from your address, not ours — so it looks professional and personal.
What's the Lifetime deal about? Is it real? ▼
100% real. We're offering the first 200 users lifetime Pro access for a one-time $49 payment. No subscriptions, no renewals. You get every Pro feature forever, including all future updates. When the 200 spots are gone, they're gone — the price goes to $5/month. We're doing this to build a community of founding members who help shape the product.
Can I customize the email templates? ▼
Right now, PaidUp auto-personalizes templates with your invoice details (client name, amount, days overdue, late fees). Full template customization is on the roadmap — and as a founding member, you get a direct say in what we build next. The current templates are based on what actually works to get paid, based on real-world follow-up strategies.
